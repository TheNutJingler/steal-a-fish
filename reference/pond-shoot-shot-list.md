# Steal a Fish — Pond Shoot Game-Reference Shot List

You're already at the pond tonight with the DJI Action 4. These take about
5 minutes total, squeezed between fishing, and give Tony real reference for
the game's river visuals + ambient sound. Do them in this order — fastest
first, skip anything that's a hassle.

1. **Slow pan across calm water** (30–60s, no talking)
   River/conveyor visual reference. Keep it steady, one slow sweep.

2. **Water color reference** (2 photos)
   One of the water in daylight, one after the light fades. Color grading
   reference for the river surface.

3. **Water meeting the bank** (1 clip, ~10s)
   Shoreline transition look — how water laps into mud/rocks/grass.

4. **Surface movement** (15s if you see it)
   Any baitfish/minnows dimpling the surface or wakes from a bass —
   movement reference for fish variants.

5. **Ambient water sound** (10s, no talking)
   Get the camera low near the water. This becomes the game's ambient
   river loop.

That's it. None of this is required — but the water tonight is the exact
vibe the game's river needs, and reference you shoot yourself beats anything
pulled off the internet.
